# Next steps (from the owner's PC playtest, Nov 2026)

Requests in the order they will be done. Screenshots from the test: Črna
na Koroškem close-up (grainy ground), a game start (pale tribe land looks
like snow), mid-game with rain (territory colour hides the terrain), and a
late game where building icons pile up and grow huge near the camera.

## Phase 1 — maps, 3D terrain and structures (first)

1. **Terrain:** smoother and more realistic; real 100-level heights
   (0 ground - 100 mountain tops) with detail. Fix all four: grainy noisy
   ground, flat snow blobs (and pale tribe land that looks like snow),
   not enough relief, too many trees.
2. **Icons:** small (about 16 px), a constant size on screen (they now
   grow near the camera); overlapping icons merge into one with a number
   until you zoom in. _(chosen: small + grouped)_
3. **Territory display:** light country tint + clear outline so the
   terrain stays visible; hovering a country shows it in full colour; a
   Settings slider sets the tint. _(chosen: light tint + outline)_
4. **Structures:** better 3D models for every building. _(done: city,
   port, factory, market, oil rig, airbase, bunker, SAM, silo)_
5. **Bug fixes** found along the way; verify everything.

## Phase 2 — weapons and power (done)

6. Weapons with bigger destruction. _(done: tiered blasts, Mk III wrecks
   buildings, lingering fires)_
7. Better side weapons: artillery, naval bombardment. _(done: Mk I-III,
   muzzle flashes)_
8. Real planes flying and bombing. _(done: new bomber and fighter models,
   contrails, cruise over mountains, bombs drop from the plane)_
9. Power tiers and upgrade systems. _(done: Mk II / Mk III per weapon, ▲
   in the weapons panel; nations upgrade too)_

## Phase 3 — nature

10. Natural disasters and volcanoes. _(chosen: volcanoes, earthquakes,
    tsunamis/floods, storms and wildfires; random but identical on every
    client, with an On/Off + frequency lobby option, off in the tutorial;
    medium strength (hurts a region, never wipes out a player); a ~10 s
    warning with a map marker and message before each one hits)_
